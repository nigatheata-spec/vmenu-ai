# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Restaurant owners and managers in Arabic-speaking markets (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Jordan, Egypt, Lebanon). They run physical venues from casual eateries to upscale dining. They use the platform mid-shift, often on mobile, under warm ambient restaurant lighting, making quick operational decisions.

## Product Purpose

Restaurant management SaaS: digital QR menus, AI-enhanced food photography, video content generation, order management, staff coordination, AI promotional suggestions. Makes modern restaurant operations possible without technical expertise.

## Brand Personality

Precise, capable, direct. The tool disappears into the task.

## Anti-references

Generic amber-on-dark "AI SaaS" landing pages. Gradient text heroes. Browser-window product mockups. "Powered by AI" pulse badges. Glassmorphism dashboard cards. Anything that looks like a Webflow template for a startup accelerator.

## Design Principles

1. Arabic-first: RTL is not an afterthought — layout and reading direction are designed for Arabic before English.
2. Earned familiarity: use standard patterns because they work, not because they're safe.
3. The tool disappears: no decorative motion, no gratuitous color, no celebration when there's nothing to celebrate.
4. Clarity at a glance: operators check this mid-shift; every screen communicates its main action immediately.
5. Restraint with purpose: one accent color, used only for primary actions and active state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Bilingual RTL/LTR throughout. High contrast for use in varying ambient light.
